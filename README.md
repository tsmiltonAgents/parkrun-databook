# parkrun, by the numbers — a data book

An independent, *Our World in Data*-style visual essay on parkrun as a global
phenomenon: ~2,900 free weekly 5K events across 21 countries since 2004.

**Read it:** the live site (GitHub Pages) — `index.html` is the Tufte-CSS edition;
`scroll.html` is the scrollytelling edition; `book.md` is the plain-markdown version.

31 figures across six chapters, every one red-teamed, each with a "how this was
made — data, maths & stats" box. Highlights: the punctuated global spread; the
sub-20 clock-stampede; the rebuilt age–performance curve; loyalty's tripling
one-and-done rate; the milestone-t-shirt quit magnet; the weather tax; the COVID
scar; the sublinear volunteer economy.

Built from public parkrun results (the complete history of Bushy parkrun plus a
worldwide event-history census) and Open-Meteo weather. Figures show only
**aggregates, never individuals**. Not affiliated with or endorsed by parkrun.
